About Me

Hi, I'm Meghan Tierney! I'm currently a Senior at UMass Amherst studying English with a minor in Sociology, and a specialization in Professional Writing and Technical Communication. After graduating, I plan to pursue opportunities in editorial publishing, content design, and digital marketing.

Currently, I am employed as an editorial and marketing intern at Restless Books, a non-profit publishing group. There, I manage both working on acquisitions in-house while also presenting new titles to the public through creative marketing initiatives. The collaborative environment of a small press allows me to gain hands-on experience while simultaneously giving me the freedom to pursue my own passion projects in publishing.

As I look ahead, I hope to continue my work in the publishing field and possibly shift my focus specifically to the editorial world and the magazine industry. My continued dedication to engaging diverse audiences with intent and clarity through multimedia creation will serve me well in these roles.
