Outlining Accessibility & Digital Design in Madcap Flare

As an undergraduate student focusing on technical writing and digital design, accessibility practices in the digital sphere are essential, no matter the type of software used. The purpose of this site is to provide an overview of digital design practices that are compliant with accessibility regulations for those in design related fields.

* This project has been created as a semester-long project for English 382: Professional Writing and Technical Communication

Work Process

Part of the modular content drafted is broken down into various team assignments, each on a specific feature of digital accessibility. This content will be featured in the final Madcap Flare website:

Team 1: Video Transcripts

Team 2: Closed Captions

Team 3: Audio Description

Team 4: Visual Formatting

Team 5: Visual Transition Effects

Team 6: Chunking, Chapters, On-Screen Markup

As a member of Team 1, we broke our content on video transcripts down into several sections and divided these equally into 3 groups. After researching and drafting our initial content, our team went through together to edit our work and ensure we created a uniform and consistent piece of content for our website. The remaining sections of the website have been created by other groups and revised as seen fit by me. Creating a cohesive website with all of these different moving parts was essential.

The creation of this product strengthened my design skills and refined my perspective on accessibility. The research, collaboration, and revision aspects of this project required dedication and time. I hope this site is as insightful to you as it has been to me. Thank you and enjoy!
