 # Outlining Accessibility & Digital Design in Madcap Flare
As an undergraduate student focusing on technical writing and digital design, accessibility practices in the digital sphere are essential, no matter the type of software used. 
## Flare Project :exclamation:
The purpose of this flare project is to provide an overview of digital design practices that are compliant with accessibility regulations. 
The deliverable for this project will be a [Madcap Flare](https://www.madcapsoftware.com/products/flare/) website with multiple pages that contextualize accessibility practices and provide specific examples. 
## Team Assignments
Part of the modular content drafted is broken down into various team assignments, each on a specific feature of digital accessibility. This content will be featured in the final *Madcap Flare* website:
+ Team 1: Video Transcripts
+ Team 2: Closed Captions
+ Team 3: Audio Description
+ Team 4: Visual Formatting
+ Team 5: Visual Transition Effects
+ Team 6: Chunking, Chapters, On-Screen Markup
  
As a member of Team 1, we broke our content on video transcripts down into several sections and divided these equally into 3 groups. After researching and drafting our initial content, our team went through together to edit our work and ensure we created a uniform and consistent piece of content for our website. 
## Takeaways
1. From this project, I will have a strong grasp on *Madcap Flare* as a tool and tangible evidence of the skills I have learned from using this software
2. So far, I have learned how to be a better and more intentional designer with considering accessibility from the initial stages of design 
3. I have developed my skills in balancing collaboration and independent work, ensuring I am holding both myself and my team accountable
